ELEPHANT FIELD NOTES

# **Ears and body size**

## **Ears**


Elephant ears help release body heat. Flapping them helps cool blood passing through their

vessels. African elephants generally have larger ears than Asian elephants.

## **Size**


Elephants are the largest living land mammals. Body size varies with species, sex, and age.

African savanna elephants are generally larger than Asian elephants.
