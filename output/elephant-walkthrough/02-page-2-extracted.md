ELEPHANT FIELD NOTES

# **Teeth and legs**

## **Teeth and tusks**


Tusks are elongated incisor teeth. Elephants use tusks for digging and defense. Inside the

mouth, molars grind plant food.


New molars move forward from the back of the jaw as older ones wear down. Tusks and

chewing teeth have different roles.

## **Four legs**


An elephant has four legs. The legs support its large body and allow it to walk. Legs provide

movement; the trunk handles food.
